# 👤 PERSONA & PRODUCT CONTEXT

## 1. Brand Identity & Overview
- **Brand Name**: Biryani Spot Chennai Dosa (Biryani and Dosa Grill)
- **Tagline**: *"Authentic Chettinad South Indian Cuisine"*
- **Cuisine Type**: Authentic Chettinad & South Indian Cuisine (Traditional spice blends, slow-cooked Dum biryanis, fermented rice crepes/dosas, homestyle gravies, sapadu thalis, and coastal delicacies).
- **Price Range**: `$$` (Affordable, family-friendly, premium quality).
- **Domains**: `biryanispotchennaidosa.com`, `chennaidosa.toast.site`

## 2. Brand Voice, Tone & Messaging
- **Voice**: Warm, authentic, welcoming, appetizing, and culturally rooted.
- **Tone**: Proud of authentic flavors, hospitality-driven, and clear.
- **Key Value Propositions**:
  - **Heritage Spices**: Freshly ground masalas and traditional Chettinad culinary techniques.
  - **Signature Specialties**: Over 20+ Biryani varieties (Thalapakatti, Vijayawada, Gongura, Goat Sukka) and 25+ Crispy Dosas & Podi Idlys.
  - **Multi-Location Convenience**: 4 locations across the Bay Area with instant online ordering, pickup, and delivery.
  - **Full-Scale Catering & Events**: Custom catering for weddings, parties, corporate events, and food truck operations.

## 3. Target Audience & Customer Personas
1. **The South Indian Food Connoisseur**: Seeks authentic Chettinad taste, traditional spice levels, and specialty items like *Killi Parotta*, *Elaneer Payasam*, and *Thalapakatti Biryani*.
2. **Local Bay Area Families & Groups**: Looking for family-sized biryani packs, weekend thalis (*Sapadu*), and child-friendly options like Butter Naan, Sweet Corn Soup, and Desserts.
3. **Busy Working Professionals**: Seeking quick weekday lunch pickups, combo meals, and frictionless online ordering via mobile.
4. **Party & Corporate Event Organizers**: Requiring bulk catering trays, customizable menus, and reliable on-time service for 20 to 500+ guests.
