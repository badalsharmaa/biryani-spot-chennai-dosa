Our Heritage

The Art Of
Authentic
South Indian Cuisine from tamil nadu & hyderabad to the bay area

Founded with a singular mission: to bring authentic, unapologetic South Indian flavors to Northern California. Rooted in the ancient spice-rich traditions of Chettinad, Tamil Nadu and the royal dum biryani kitchens of Hyderabad, our journey began with creating a dining sanctuary where time-honored slow-cooking methods meet fresh, premium California ingredients.

Across four Bay Area locations in Dublin, Milpitas, Livermore, and Concord, Biryani Spot Chennai Dosa has become a cherished destination for food lovers seeking true Dum Biryani and golden fermented Dosas.

Culinary Philosophy

“We do not cut corners. Every masala is freshly ground, every handi is sealed with dough, and every dosa is fermented naturally.”

02
The Dum Pukht Tradition

The Mastery Of
Dum Pukht &
Crispy Dosas slow-steamed perfection & golden fermented crepes

“True Dum Biryani is an art of patience, aroma, and delicate heat control.” Basmati rice is layered with marinated meats, caramelized onions, and whole spices, then sealed hermetically with dough in heavy vessels. As the trapped steam circulates, every grain absorbs saffron and rich masalas. Alongside, our dosas are spread thin across roaring cast-iron tawas from batter fermented overnight, delivering an irresistible crispy texture and unmatched golden aroma.

Together, our culinary team blends generational regional recipes with attentive modern hospitality, creating an unforgettable sensory journey in every meal.

Chef Senthil
Kumar Executive Head Chef

With over two decades mastering Chettinad and Tamil regional heritage recipes, Chef Senthil oversees our master kitchen, ensuring every gravy, kurma, and curry achieves its iconic balance of black pepper, fennel, and stone-ground spices.

Specializing in royal Hyderabadi Dum Pukht biryani, Chef Farooq manages the delicate precision of layered basmati, clay handi sealing, and saffron infusion, perfecting the long-grain aroma that defines our signature dishes.
